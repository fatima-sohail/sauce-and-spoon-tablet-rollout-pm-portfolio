## My notes:
## Key Takeaways

- After Sauce & Spoon confirms how many tablets are needed, Seydou will place the order with Terrific Tablets.
- Shipping usually takes about one week.
- Seydou does not expect inventory to be a problem because Terrific Tablets is releasing an upgraded tablet model before Sauce & Spoon needs the devices.
- Seydou has seen shipping delays on another project. That delay pushed installation and the rest of the project schedule back by a few days.
- Peta should leave an extra two to three days in the schedule in case shipping is delayed.
- Broken tablets do not happen often, but they are possible.
- If a tablet arrives broken, Terrific Tablets can send a replacement overnight.


## Risks and Mitigation

| Risk | Potential Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Tablet shipment delay | Installation and later tasks may be delayed by several days. | Include a two- to three-day schedule buffer between expected delivery and installation. |
| Inventory shortage | Tablet order could be delayed beyond the standard one-week shipping estimate. | Confirm inventory availability with Terrific Tablets before finalizing the installation date. |
| Broken or defective devices | Installation or launch may be affected if devices do not work. | Inspect devices upon delivery and request overnight replacement devices immediately if needed. |
