# Sauce & Spoon Tablet Rollout — Project Task List

## Milestone: Get Tablets

- Research tablet models that are most efficient for our restaurant, including researching tablet features, comparing tablet pricing, and confirming compatible operating systems.
- Choose a vendor to buy tablets from and schedule a meeting with them.
- Create contracts and statements of work for vendors.
- Generate quotes and review costs.
- Order tablets.

## Milestone: Tablet Installation Complete

- Schedule an electrician to install the tablets and connect them to the kitchen via Wi-Fi.
- Install tablets in the bar area at the Downtown location.
- Install tablets in the bar area at the North location.
- Hire developers to integrate the new tablet software with existing systems that manage orders, pricing, checks, and table/waitlist tracking.
- Hire developers to build an offline-mode feature so staff can continue taking and closing orders, sending orders to the kitchen, and processing offline payments if connectivity is lost.
- Test the new software.

## Milestone: Training Complete

- Create a plan for manager training.
- Schedule training for managers first.
- Schedule staff meetings with general managers at each location to discuss anything that could affect staff tips, in order to gain staff buy-in.
- Schedule training for staff, led by managers at each location.

## Milestone: Launch Day Complete

- Create a launch-day plan, including staffing and troubleshooting procedures.
- Hire a graphic designer to design a customized tablet menu layout that resembles the printed menu.
- Create marketing ads to inform guests about available coupons.
- Connect the Sauce & Spoon marketing team with Terrific Tablets for branding.
- Invite friends and family to complete a test run of the tablets.
- Create a post-dining survey to assess customer satisfaction.
- Implement the post-dining survey to assess customer satisfaction.
- Train staff to update the menu.
- Create a feedback loop for staff once they begin using the new system.

## Milestone: Hire Cooks

- Create a job description and eligibility criteria for line cooks.
- Advertise two line-cook positions on the restaurant’s careers page, LinkedIn, Indeed, and local newspapers.
- Interview candidates.
- Fill the two line-cook positions.

## Milestone: Recurring Updates

- Regularly update tablet software to improve performance and security.
- Maintain the feedback loop for staff using the tablet system.
