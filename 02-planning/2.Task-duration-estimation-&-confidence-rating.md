## Estimation Method
### Three-Point Estimating Method

Task estimates were created using the three-point estimation method.

- **Optimistic estimate:** The task is completed under best-case conditions with few or no delays.
- **Most likely estimate:** The task is completed under normal working conditions.
- **Pessimistic estimate:** The task is completed with reasonable risks, delays, or issues.

The PERT formula was used to calculate the estimated duration:

\[
\text{Estimated Duration} = \frac{O + 4M + P}{6}
\]

Where:

- \(O\) = Optimistic estimate
- \(M\) = Most likely estimate
- \(P\) = Pessimistic estimate

**Planning assumption:** One workday equals eight hours.

## Confidence Ratings

Confidence ratings show how certain the project team is about each time estimate.

| Rating | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| High | The work is familiar, clearly defined, and has few dependencies. | Uploading menu and coupon content after mock-ups are approved. |
| Medium | The work is understood but may be affected by scheduling or outside dependencies. | Electrician installation or tablet shipping. |
| Low | The task has significant uncertainty, technical complexity, or external dependencies. | POS update, system integration, or offline-mode development. |
