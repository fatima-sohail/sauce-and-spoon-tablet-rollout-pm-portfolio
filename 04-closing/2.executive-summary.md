# Tablet Rollout Impact Report

## Overview

This impact report summarizes the outcomes of the Sauce & Spoon tabletop tablet rollout. It was prepared for senior stakeholders and potential investors to communicate the project’s purpose, key results, lessons learned, and recommended next steps for future restaurant rollouts.

## Executive Summary

The Sauce & Spoon tablet rollout was designed to create a faster, easier guest dining experience while improving restaurant operations, increasing guest capacity, and reducing food waste. Following the test launch, the project team used customer and staff feedback to simplify tablet navigation, clarify card and cash payment options, strengthen staff training, and introduce a pre-service tablet-testing checklist.

The rollout achieved key operational results: table-turn time was reduced by 30 minutes, tablet checkout remained at one minute or less, customer-reported technical issues fell below 5%, Downtown daily guest count increased by 20%, and food waste was reduced by 25%. The project also demonstrated that tablet technology alone was not enough to improve service flow; waitstaff training, guest pacing, and clear payment instructions were essential to achieving the final results.

Going forward, Sauce & Spoon should continue monitoring tablet reliability, order accuracy, customer satisfaction, ticket times, and food waste. The project team should use the lessons, actual costs, scheduling data, and operational improvements from this rollout to plan and manage the next restaurant-location deployment.
