## Notes
## What Went Well

- Tablets were successfully installed and functioning at both the Downtown and North locations.
- Guests adapted well to using the tablets, and payments generally worked as expected.
- Kitchen ticket flow was smooth and easy for kitchen staff to track.
- Technical issues during POS integration were identified and resolved quickly.
- Weekly vendor calls helped the team stay on schedule and manage task dependencies.
- The customer satisfaction survey produced meaningful data, and most satisfaction standards were met.
- Kitchen staff at the Downtown location proactively implemented operational improvements based on the new ticket flow.

## Areas for Improvement

- Table-turn time did not decrease as much as expected at either location.
- Some food orders were still sent back, indicating ongoing order-accuracy issues.
- Birthday club participation was lower than expected.
- Tablet implementation took longer than planned due to unaccounted-for staff vacation time.
- The team encountered unplanned internal operational issues once implementation and training began.
- There is a knowledge gap between front-of-house and back-of-house staff regarding each other's workflows.
- Waitstaff training may need to be more comprehensive or split into multiple sessions.
- Back-of-house staff need additional support to prepare for higher order volume at full launch.

## Action Items

- **Peta:** Prioritize further analysis of returned food orders and ticket accuracy.
- **Seydou:** Investigate whether a technical issue is contributing to ticket-accuracy problems.
- **Seydou:** Update the website and tablet content to clarify the birthday club program and grow participation.
- **Peta:** Update the process manual to document solutions for recurring technical issues.
- **Peta:** Recommend continuing weekly vendor check-in calls for future rollouts.
- **Team:** Review each location's operational history earlier in future project planning.
- **Team:** Account for staff vacation schedules when planning implementation timelines.
- **Zane:** Help back-of-house staff prepare to scale operations for higher order volume.
- **Larissa and Zane:** Facilitate cross-training or shared learning between front-of-house and back-of-house teams.
- **Alex:** Evaluate whether waitstaff training should be expanded or split into two sessions covering both tablet use and broader service operations.
