# Sauce & Spoon Test Launch

## Customer Survey Results

Sauce & Spoon conducted a survey with the 50 customers who participated in the test launch. During the test launch, customers used the newly implemented tablets as part of a typical restaurant experience. Customers received a digital survey after their visit.

The results below show the number of respondents who selected each response and the percentage of the 50 total participants where applicable.

---

## 1. Orders Placed During Visit

**Question:** During your visit, what did you order? Choose all that apply.

| Order Type | Respondents | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Drinks | 28 | 56% |
| Appetizers | 41 | 82% |
| Dinner | 50 | 100% |
| Dessert | 35 | 70% |

| Response | Respondents | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
