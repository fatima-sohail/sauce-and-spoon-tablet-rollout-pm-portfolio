# Quality and Evaluation

## Overview

This document summarizes the quality standards, evaluation questions, and evaluation indicators developed to assess the success of the Sauce & Spoon tablet test launch.

The quality standards focus on customer satisfaction by improving order accuracy, service efficiency, checkout speed, tablet reliability, table-turn time, and customer wait time.

## Purpose

The purpose of this evaluation is to determine whether the tablet test launch meets Sauce & Spoon’s customer-satisfaction quality standards and to identify areas that need improvement before a full implementation.

Evaluation questions examine the outcomes, effectiveness, and customer experience associated with the tablet ordering system. Evaluation indicators identify the specific data that will be collected to answer those questions.

## Evaluation Questions

The following evaluation questions will be used to assess the outcomes, impact, and effectiveness of the tablet test launch:

1. Is tablet ordering improving order accuracy, and is Sauce & Spoon achieving the 98% order-accuracy target?

2. Are the tablets helping create a faster and more efficient dining experience by improving appetizer ticket time, entrée ticket time, table-turn time, checkout time, and customer wait time?

3. Are customers satisfied with the tablet ordering and checkout experience, and what technical or usability issues should be addressed before a full launch?

---

## Evaluation Indicators

The following information will be collected to answer the evaluation questions:

- Percentage of orders prepared and delivered correctly.
- Number, type, and frequency of order errors, including incorrect entrées, missed modifications, incorrect sides, and preparation issues.
- Average appetizer ticket time and the percentage of appetizers delivered within 8 minutes.
- Average entrée ticket time and the percentage of entrées delivered within 15 minutes.
- Average table-turn time compared with the pre-tablet baseline.
- Average checkout time and the percentage of checkouts completed in 1 minute or less.
- Customer survey ratings for checkout speed, ease of use, and security.
- Number and percentage of customers reporting tablet technical issues.
- Types of reported tablet issues, including freezing, glitches, failed payments, or required tablet reboots.
- Tablet uptime rate and the number of technical-support requests.
- Average customer wait time before seating and the percentage of customers seated within 10 minutes.
- Customer feedback about tablet navigation, ordering, payment confidence, service preferences, and the overall dining experience.

## Data Collection Methods

| Data Needed | Collection Method |
|---|---|
| Order accuracy | Review POS tickets, kitchen records, customer feedback, and reported order corrections. |
| Ticket times | Use POS timestamps to compare order-placement time with food-delivery time. |
| Table-turn time | Compare table seating and payment-completion timestamps before and during the test launch. |
| Checkout time | Use tablet and payment-system timestamps from checkout initiation through successful payment completion. |
| Tablet reliability | Track technical-issue reports, reboot requests, support tickets, and observed tablet downtime. |
| Customer satisfaction | Collect digital customer-survey responses after the dining experience. |
| Customer wait time | Track guest arrival or waitlist time and table-seating time. |

---

## Success Criteria

The tablet test launch will be considered successful when the project demonstrates progress toward the following outcomes:

- At least 98% of orders are prepared and delivered correctly.
- Appetizers are delivered in an average of 8 minutes or less.
- Entrées are delivered in an average of 12–15 minutes.
- Average table-turn time is reduced by 30 minutes.
- Customers complete tablet checkout in 1 minute or less on average.
- Tablets function properly at least 97% of the time.
- Customers wait 10 minutes or less on average before being seated.
- Customer feedback shows that the tablet system is easy to use, efficient, and secure.
