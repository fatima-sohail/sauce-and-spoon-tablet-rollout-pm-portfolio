## Presentation Overview

This presentation summarizes the evaluation results from the Sauce & Spoon tablet test launch. It is intended for high-level stakeholders and potential investors.

The test launch included 50 friends-and-family guests who used the tabletop tablets during their dining experience and completed a post-visit survey.

---

## Milestone Reached

### Tablet Test Launch Completed

- Tested the tabletop tablet experience with 50 friends-and-family guests.
- Collected guest feedback through a post-visit tablet survey.
- Evaluated customer satisfaction, tablet usability, order accuracy, checkout experience, service efficiency and technical performance.
- Used survey responses to identify tablet-rollout strengths and improvement opportunities.

---

## Evaluation Focus

The project team evaluated whether the test launch met key customer-satisfaction goals:

- Were guests satisfied with the tablet experience?
- Did the tablets support accurate orders, faster service, and efficient checkout?
- Did guests experience technical issues while using the tablets?
- What improvements are needed before expanding the tablet rollout?

---

## Key Finding

### 72% of Guests Reported a Better Dining Experience

**72% of survey respondents said that the tablet improved their dining experience.**

This result indicates that most test-launch guests responded positively to the tabletop tablet experience and supports continuing the rollout with targeted improvements.

| Survey Response | Respondents | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| The tablet improved the dining experience | 36 | 72% |
| The tablet did not improve the dining experience | 14 | 28% |
| Total survey respondents | 50 | 100% |

> Note: Use the survey question, *“The tabletop tablet improved my dining experience today,”* as the source for this result.

---

## Recommendation 1: Improve Tablet Reliability

Technical reliability should be improved before the full pilot or broader rollout.

- Review every technical issue reported during the test launch.
- Identify recurring causes of frozen, slow, or glitchy tablet behavior.
- Test tablet connectivity, ordering functions, payment processing, and software performance before each service period.
- Create a process for staff to quickly reboot or replace a malfunctioning tablet.
- Track tablet technical issues weekly to confirm that the project meets the quality standard of tablets functioning properly at least 97% of the time.

---

## Recommendation 2: Strengthen Guest Support

Guest guidance and staff support should be improved to make the tablet experience easier for all customers.

- Update tablet instructions based on customer feedback.
- Use clear, short on-screen guidance for ordering, modifying menu items, paying and requesting waiter assistance.
- Train staff to identify and assist guests who need help using the tablets.
- Maintain a waiter-service option for customers who prefer traditional interaction or need to pay with cash.
- Collect feedback during the next pilot phase to confirm that navigation, ordering, and checkout are easy to use.
