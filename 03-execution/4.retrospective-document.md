# Retrospective Document

## Overview

This retrospective captures feedback from Sauce & Spoon customers and the project team after the tablet test launch. The feedback identifies what went well, what needs improvement, supporting evidence, and recommended actions before the official launch and future rollouts.

## Key Retrospective Findings

### What Went Well

- Customers responded positively to the tablet experience, with 72% rating it Good or Great.
- The checkout experience was a strength, with 82% of guests finding it quick, easy, and secure.
- Most guests—88%—reported no tablet technical issues.
- Tablets were installed and operating at both restaurant locations.
- Kitchen staff reported that tablet ticket flow was manageable and easier to track.
- Weekly vendor communication and customer survey data supported project coordination and informed decision-making.

### Improvement Priorities

- Improve order accuracy, which was 72% during the test launch and below the 98% quality standard.
- Reduce customer wait times, as 54% of guests waited more than 15 minutes to be seated.
- Improve Birthday Club messaging and enrollment, since only 16% of guests signed up.
- Identify why table-turn time did not improve despite successful tablet installation and on-time food delivery.
- Include location-specific operational needs, staff availability, vacation schedules, and schedule buffers in future rollout planning.
- Strengthen front-of-house and back-of-house collaboration through more complete and cross-functional training.
