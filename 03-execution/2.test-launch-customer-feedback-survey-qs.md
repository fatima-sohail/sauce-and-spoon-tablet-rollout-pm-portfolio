# Customer Feedback Survey

## Overview

This document contains the customer feedback survey questions created for the Sauce & Spoon tablet test launch.

The survey is designed to collect measurable customer feedback related to the project’s quality standards, including order accuracy, ticket times, checkout speed, tablet functionality, tablet usability, wait times, and overall customer satisfaction.

📊 [View the Survey Questions in the Project Plan](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1DfGyOcvBIgpbKJHzchE5Mk2Y0h8uXZN4oNgXZlUVsR4/edit?gid=1890271995#gid=1890271995)

---

## Survey Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to evaluate the customer experience during the Sauce & Spoon tablet test launch.

Survey responses will help the project team determine whether the tablet system is meeting customer-satisfaction quality standards and identify improvements needed before a larger rollout.

The survey includes yes/no, true/false, multiple-choice, scaled, and open-ended questions.

---

## Survey Questions

| # | Survey Question | Question Type | Answer Options |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Was your order correct when it arrived? | Yes/No | Yes; No; I did not use a tablet |
| 2 | About how long did you wait for your appetizer after placing the order? | Multiple choice | Less than 5 minutes; 5–8 minutes; 9–12 minutes; More than 12 minutes; I did not order an appetizer |
| 3 | About how long did you wait for your entrée after placing the order? | Multiple choice | Less than 10 minutes; 10–12 minutes; 13–15 minutes; More than 15 minutes; I did not order an entrée |
| 4 | How long did it take to complete checkout or payment? | Multiple choice | 1 minute or less; 2–3 minutes; 4–5 minutes; More than 5 minutes; I did not use the tablet for checkout |
| 5 | How satisfied were you with the checkout process? | Scaled | Very satisfied; Satisfied; Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied; Dissatisfied; Very dissatisfied |
| 6 | Did you experience a technical issue while using the tablet? | Multiple choice | No; Yes, the tablet was slow; Yes, the tablet froze or stopped working; Yes, an order or payment issue occurred; Other |
| 7 | Did the tablet work properly throughout your visit? | Yes/No | Yes; No; I did not use a tablet |
| 8 | How easy or difficult was it to use the tablet? | Scaled | Very easy; Somewhat easy; Neither easy nor difficult; Somewhat difficult; Very difficult |
| 9 | About how long did you wait to be seated today? | Multiple choice | 10 minutes or less; 11–20 minutes; 21–30 minutes; More than 30 minutes; I did not wait to be seated |
| 10 | Overall, how satisfied were you with your dining experience today? | Scaled | Very satisfied; Satisfied; Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied; Dissatisfied; Very dissatisfied |
| 11 | The tabletop tablet improved my dining experience today. | True/False | True; False |
| 12 | What, if anything, made the tablet difficult to use during your visit? | Open-ended | Open-ended |
| 13 | What is one change that would improve your ordering or checkout experience? | Open-ended | Open-ended |

## Quality Standards Measured

| Quality Standard | Survey Questions Used |
|---|---|
| Achieve at least 98% order accuracy | 1 |
| Deliver appetizers in 8 minutes or less | 2 |
| Deliver entrées in 12–15 minutes | 3 |
| Reduce average table-turn time | 2, 3, 4, and 9 |
| Complete tablet checkout in 1 minute or less | 4 and 5 |
| Ensure tablets function properly at least 97% of the time | 6, 7, 8, 11, and 12 |
| Reduce customer wait time before seating to 10 minutes or less | 9 |
| Improve overall customer satisfaction | 5, 8, 10, 11, 12, and 13 |

---

## Evaluation Data Collected

Survey responses will provide data on:

- The percentage of customers who received the correct order.
- Customer-reported appetizer and entrée wait times.
- Customer-reported checkout duration and checkout satisfaction.
- The number and type of tablet technical issues customers experience.
- Customer ratings of tablet usability and functionality.
- Customer-reported wait time before being seated.
- Overall customer satisfaction with the dining experience.
- Customer feedback about tablet usability, ordering, and checkout improvements.
