## Executive Summary

Sauce & Spoon is a local restaurant chain specializing in scratch-to-table cuisine and committed to partnering with local farmers and suppliers.

### Company Mission and Objectives

Sauce & Spoon exists to enrich our community:

- Make fresh, quick food for the working families of our community
- Prioritize our customer needs and wants
- Run an efficient, profitable business model so we can continue to grow in our community
- Support local businesses through our sourcing and partnerships

The restaurant group is growing in annual sales by **28% each year** and wants to meet demand by improving current restaurant operations. The greatest areas of opportunity to reduce costs include:

- Staffing
- Order size and amount
- Improving customer satisfaction and retention
- Providing timely, quality service

To address these needs, we propose implementing a guest-facing digital menu and point-of-sale (POS) system. This initiative will take the form of a fully integrated tablet system located at each table to offer guests a seamless ordering experience while providing the restaurant with a more effective ticketing system.

## Opportunity Statement

In our first three years of business, Sauce & Spoon was able to exceed revenue goals and keep up with customer demand without letting quality suffer. However, we faced staff burnout, which led to high employee turnover, including a **50% front-of-house reduction**. We also risked profitability due to human error in ticketing and little-to-no focus on new employee onboarding.

Today, we have increased the number of restaurant locations, and customer sales continue to grow. However, we have failed to create more efficient procedures or address issues of quality.

Internal data shows that:

- The time it takes customers to receive their orders has increased by **20%**
- Negative customer-satisfaction reviews are connected to delays in service
- Despite increased sales, profit margins have remained flat year over year

We are confident this initiative will be well received by customers and will help reduce errors and sunk costs.

## Project Overview

### What?

Roll out tabletop menu tablets to assist customers with quick, easy ordering options.

### Why?

Implementing menu tablets at tables and digitizing the ordering process will:

- Speed up service and related restaurant processes
- Allow Sauce & Spoon to turn tables more quickly and serve more guests
- Provide clear data points to track metrics and support restaurant success
- Improve the ticketing process and reduce potential ordering errors
- Help Sauce & Spoon keep up with the growing digital presence in the restaurant industry
- Provide customers with a familiar digital experience
