# Sauce & Spoon Tabletop Tablet Pilot

## Project Charter

**Date:** August 11, 2026

---

## Project Summary
Sauce & Spoon will pilot tabletop menu tablets in the bar areas of its North and Downtown restaurant locations, beginning in April of Q2. The tablets will allow guests to view menus, place orders, receive promotions or coupons, and potentially add appetizers, specialty drinks, or other menu items to their checks.

The pilot is intended to improve operational efficiency, support higher sales and product mix, reduce food waste, and improve the guest experience. The project team will measure results through the end of June, including table-turn time, daily guest counts, food waste, appetizer sales, average check value, revenue changes, and kitchen staffing capacity.
