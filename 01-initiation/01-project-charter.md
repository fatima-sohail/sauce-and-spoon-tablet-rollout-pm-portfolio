Sauce and Spoon
Project Charter: Tablet Pilot Project Charter

DATE: 08/11/26
Project Summary:
Sauce & Spoon will pilot tabletop menu tablets in the bar areas of its North and Downtown restaurant locations, beginning in April of Q2. The tablets will allow guests to view menus, place orders, receive promotions or coupons, and potentially add appetizers, specialty drinks, or other menu items to their checks.
The pilot is intended to improve operational efficiency, support higher sales and product mix, reduce food waste, and improve the guest experience. The project team will measure results through the end of June, including table-turn time, daily guest counts, food waste, appetizer sales, average check value, revenue changes, and kitchen staffing capacity.

Project Goals:
To increase our product mix by upselling appetizers or promoting certain entrees.
Decrease average turn table time by 30%.
Increasing our average daily guest counts by 10%
To reduce food waste
Reduce number of negative customer reviews.
Launch timeline — pilot to begin at the start of Q2(April)

Project Deliverables:
to roll out the pilot in the bar in both locations
A plan to train the staff on the new system
Tablet vendor/package selection: to support menu add-ons and coupons
Clear data points to track metrics: track data around how the tablets impact table turn time and changes in revenue?
POS and host software integration plan —ensure tablets connect properly with existing systems that manage orders, pricing, checks, and table/waitlist tracking
