# Sauce & Spoon Tabletop Tablet Pilot

## Project Charter

**Date:** August 11, 2026

---

## Project Summary
Sauce & Spoon will pilot tabletop menu tablets in the bar areas of its North and Downtown restaurant locations, beginning in April of Q2. The tablets will allow guests to view menus, place orders, receive promotions or coupons, and potentially add appetizers, specialty drinks, or other menu items to their checks.

The pilot is intended to improve operational efficiency, support higher sales and product mix, reduce food waste, and improve the guest experience. The project team will measure results through the end of June, including table-turn time, daily guest counts, food waste, appetizer sales, average check value, revenue changes, and kitchen staffing capacity.

## Scope and Exclusion

### In-Scope

- Pilot tablet roll out in bar area only in both locations: North and Downtown
- Researching, selecting and coordinating with a tablet vendor/package that supports menu add-ons, coupons and promotions.
- Configuring tablet menus, item pricing, promotions, coupon offers and upsell recommendations for appetizers and specialty beverages.
- Integrating the tablets with the existing POS, order-management, kitchen-ticket, host and table/waitlist systems.
- Developing and delivering staff training for employees who will use, support or manage the tablet-ordering process.
- Establishing a data-tracking plan to measure average check value, appetizer sales, table-turn time, food waste, daily guest counts, revenue changes and customer feedback.
- Monitoring back-of-house staffing capacity, payroll and kitchen workload during the pilot.
- Opening two part-time line-cook roles as part of the pilot’s kitchen-resourcing plan.
- Launching the pilot in April and evaluating results through the end of Q2, including a final pilot evaluation report and expansion recommendations.

### Out-of-Scope

- Expansion to other Sauce & Spoon restaurant locations during this pilot.
- Reallocation of front-of-house payroll to back-of-house payroll beyond pilot monitoring and the two part-time line-cook roles.
- Changes to Sauce & Spoon’s customer return policy.

## Benefits & Costs

### Benefits

- Improve the guest ordering experience and reduce wait times.
- Increase revenue (via introducing coupons, more menu items and serving more guests).
- Reduction in food waste
- Gathering data on sales, customer behavior, table-turn time, revenue, and kitchen capacity will help future expansion.
