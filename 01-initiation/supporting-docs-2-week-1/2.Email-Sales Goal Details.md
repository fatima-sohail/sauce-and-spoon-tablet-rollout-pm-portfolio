## Email 2

**From:** Gilly  
**To:** Peta  
**Subject:** Re: Sales goal details

Hi Peta,

The confirmed goals look good to me!

As for increasing appetizer sales, this one’s a little tricky. My restaurant is in the business district, so we get lots of groups joining us for happy hour after work, and they tend to just order appetizers and drinks. Currently, about two-thirds of the parties that join us after 4:00 PM order appetizers, and many of them order multiple appetizers.

However, I do not think the tablets are likely to have a measurable impact on appetizer sales at my location. Servers will suggest appetizers and provide coupons, but most guests are already planning on ordering them, so I do not think it will matter much. Maybe we should remove this as an overall goal?

Thanks,  
**Gilly**

## Email 3

**From:** Alex  
**To:** Peta  
**Subject:** Re: Sales goal details

Hi Peta,

The confirmed goals look good to me, too.

For appetizer sales, the location I manage attracts more dinner guests. They do not order appetizers as consistently. At dinner time, I would estimate that about 20% of tables order appetizers before their entrees.

I would like to propose a larger increase, such as 20%, but I am not sure it is reasonable to expect that increase from the tablets alone.

Thanks,  
**Alex**

## Email 4

**From:** Peta  
**To:** Deanna, Gilly, Alex  
**Subject:** Appetizer sales goal recommendation

Hi all,

Based on feedback, we are going to keep the goal to increase appetizer sales at both restaurants because it supports the company-wide goal of increasing product mix.

Alex requested a more aggressive target, while Gilly noted that the tablets may not have a significant impact on appetizer sales at the North location. To reflect each location’s sales patterns while supporting the overall business objective, I propose:

- **15% average increase in appetizer sales overall**
- **10% increase at the North location**
- **20% increase at the Downtown location**

This approach keeps the goals relevant to each location’s unique trends while still supporting the larger company goal.

Thanks,  
**Peta**
