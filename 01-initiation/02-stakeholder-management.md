# Stakeholder Management

## Stakeholder Analysis Approach

This stakeholder analysis was completed after the project plan was drafted to identify the people who may affect, be affected by, or have an interest in the project.

Each stakeholder was assessed based on:

- **Power:** The level of influence the stakeholder has over project decisions, resources, scope, approval, implementation, or overall project outcomes.
- **Interest:** The degree to which the stakeholder’s work, responsibilities, needs, or outcomes may be affected by the project.

Power and interest were rated as **High (H), Medium (M),** or **Low (L)**. Additional relevant information, such as stakeholder concerns, communication preferences, responsibilities, and potential impact on the project, was documented in the stakeholder analysis notes and project charter where appropriate.

## Power–Interest Grid

Stakeholders were placed on a Power–Interest Grid based on their assessed level of power and interest. Their position on the grid helps determine the appropriate stakeholder-management approach.

| Power and Interest Level | Stakeholder Management Approach |
|---|---|
| High power / High interest | **Manage closely.** These stakeholders are the highest priority. Engage them regularly, involve them in key decisions, seek feedback, and keep them informed of major project progress, risks, and changes. |
| High power / Low interest | **Keep satisfied.** Provide enough information to maintain support, especially when decisions or changes may affect the project. Avoid unnecessary detail or excessive communication. |
| Low power / High interest | **Keep informed.** Share relevant updates, listen for concerns, and involve these stakeholders when their operational knowledge or feedback can improve the project. |
| Low power / Low interest | **Monitor.** Maintain awareness of these stakeholders and communicate only when necessary. Avoid overloading them with project information. |
| Medium power and/or Medium interest | **Engage as appropriate.** Place stakeholders between the relevant grid categories and adjust communication based on their role, level of impact, and the project phase. |

## Communication and Engagement Plan

The project manager will use the stakeholder analysis throughout the project to determine:

- Who needs to approve decisions or project changes
- Who should be consulted before key decisions are made
- Who needs regular progress updates
- Who may need targeted communication, training, or support
- Who may raise concerns, create risks, or help remove project barriers
- How often each stakeholder should receive project information
- It will also help during negotiations if the need arises.

The stakeholder analysis will be reviewed and updated when project scope, stakeholder responsibilities, risks, or communication needs change.
